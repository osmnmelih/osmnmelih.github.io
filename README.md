## Featured Projects

- **Legend of Cygnus:** 3D gamified microlearning simulation engineered in Unreal Engine 5 & C++ featuring custom HUDs, real-time Niagara VFX, and physics-driven mechanics.
- **Campus Orientation:** GPS-powered mobile exploration & onboarding platform engineered with React 18, TanStack Router, Leaflet, and Supabase for the official M.Sc. Educational Technology Orientation Day at Saarland University.
- **Antigravity 2.0:** Outdoor endurance training & location-based WebAR platform with a diegetic cockpit HUD developed for Dr. Kristin Altmeyer's course.
- **Ace Your Interview:** Interactive branching scenario training module built in Articulate Storyline 360.
- **EduCon:** Cross-platform on-demand peer tutoring & mentorship app built with Flutter, Node.js, and PostgreSQL.
